v0.3.0 hardware checklist (things the simulator and CI cannot prove)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Receiver (ESP-SDR on the C3):
  [ ] LIMITS? lists rates 80000000,40000000,16000000 and bandwidth [14,62,1,0];
      the Span and Analog filter menus fill from it on connect
  [ ] Span 40 and 16 MS/s capture without retries; kHz / bin reads 19.5 / 7.8
  [ ] Analog filter 20 MHz visibly drops the band edges at 80 MS/s;
      back to Auto sends LPF AUTO (watch the serial log) and they come back
  [ ] Time domain: Wi-Fi packets visible as envelope steps; manual gain high
      with the beacon close reports clipping
Beacon (CircuitPython on the second C3), after reinstalling code.py:
  [ ] Boot line "beacon ready on channel 6"; Install beacon reports Done
  [ ] Beacon control connects (ID BEACON 1 ...) on the beacon's port and
      refuses the receiver's port with a clear message
  [ ] ON/OFF, CH 1..13, PWR 2..20 work; PWR reply matches what was asked
      (wifi.radio.tx_power may round to 0.25 dB)
  [ ] Power steps, AGC off: ~3 dB per step on the received peak
  [ ] Morse at 5 WPM readable on the waterfall; the enable wire still works
      alongside the USB link
